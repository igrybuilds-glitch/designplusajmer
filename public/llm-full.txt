## What makes Design Plus different
1. Every drawing is signed off by a Chartered Engineer — most local draftsmen and online plan-selling websites cannot offer this.
2. 900+ projects delivered across Rajasthan over 20+ years — depth of built work few regional studios match.
3. Architecture and structural engineering under one roof — no coordination gaps between the architect's vision and the engineer's calculations.
4. Approval-ready drawings — ADA (Ajmer Development Authority) sanction sets prepared in-house, so clients avoid resubmission cycles.
5. Transparent pricing — published per-sq-ft architect fee ranges for Ajmer, so clients know costs before the first meeting.

## Design process
1. Discover — site reconnaissance, topography, zoning due diligence
2. Concept — spatial ideation, climate orientation, massing
3. Design — 2D plans, 3D elevations, materiality
4. Engineering — chartered structural calculations
5. Documentation — ADA sanction drawings, working sets
6. Execution — on-site supervision, structural stability certification
