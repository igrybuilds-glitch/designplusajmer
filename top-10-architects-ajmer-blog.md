## How we ranked them

## How to choose *your* architect

1. **Match the studio to the project.** A interiors-first firm isn't the right pick for a load-bearing structure, and vice versa.
2. **Ask for structural drawings, not just 3D views.** Pretty renders don't keep a building standing.
3. **Visit one live site.** Any serious architect will show you current work.
4. **Get the scope in writing.** What's included — drawings, site visits, material selection — should be on paper before money moves.
