# How to Plan Your Dream Home in Ajmer: A Step-by-Step Guide

## Step 1: Define your real requirements

Before meeting any architect, write down: number of bedrooms, joint or nuclear family, work-from-home needs, parking for how many vehicles, future expansion (another floor later?), and your honest budget range. Vague briefs get vague designs.

## Step 2: Understand your plot

Plot size, orientation (east-facing commands a premium for a reason — light and Vaastu), soil type, water level, and approach road width all shape what can be built. In Ajmer's climate, **west-facing walls need special treatment** against harsh afternoon sun.

## Step 3: Check legal & approval requirements

Verify the title, check the plot is in a valid residential zone, and understand the setback/FAR rules. Your architect should handle the naksha approval process with the local authority.

## Step 4: Choose the right architect

Look for: a real portfolio of built (not just rendered) projects, transparent fee structure, structural engineering included (not outsourced blindly), and someone who listens before sketching. Ask for 2–3 client references you can actually call.

## Step 5: Concept design & 3D elevation

Insist on seeing **3D elevations before construction starts**. A 3D view catches design mistakes that 2D plans hide — and it's far cheaper to change a render than a wall.

## Step 6: Detailed working drawings & BOQ

Floor plans, electrical layouts, plumbing drawings, and an item-wise bill of quantities. This is your financial control document — never start construction without it.

## Step 7: Construction with supervision

Whether you hire a contractor or go design-build, **site supervision by your architect** is what keeps quality on track. Agree on stage-wise payment linked to completed milestones, never large advances.

## Step 8: Handover & snag list

Walk through every room before final payment. Note every defect — paint, fittings, seepage — and get them fixed. A professional firm hands over with documentation: drawings, warranties, paint codes.
