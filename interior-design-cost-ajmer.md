# Interior Design Cost in Ajmer (2026): Per Sq Ft Rates & What's Included

## Indicative interior rates in Ajmer (2026)

| Scope | Indicative range per sq ft* |
|---|---|
| Design only (drawings + 3D views) | ₹75 – ₹150 |
| Design + execution (mid-range materials) | ₹400 – ₹800 |
| Premium / luxury interiors | ₹900 – ₹1,500+ |

*Indicative market ranges, not a quotation.

## Room-wise: where the money goes

- **Modular kitchen:** typically ₹1.5–₹4 lakh depending on size, shutter material, and accessories. The biggest single-room cost in most homes.
- **Wardrobes:** ₹1,200–₹2,500 per sq ft of wardrobe elevation, depending on finish (laminate vs acrylic vs veneer).
- **Living/dining:** false ceiling, lighting, wall treatments, and furniture — the most variable head.
- **Bedrooms:** wardrobes + paneling + lighting form the bulk.
- **Bathrooms:** sanitaryware and tiling grades swing costs widely.

## Design fee vs execution — don't confuse them

- **Design fee** pays for drawings, 3D views, and material selection.
- **Execution** is the actual carpentry, electrical, and finishing work.
- "Free design with execution" offers hide the design cost inside inflated execution rates. Item-wise quotes keep everyone honest.

## 5 ways to control interior budgets

1. **Prioritize the kitchen and master bedroom** — highest daily-use impact.
2. **Choose finishes by zone** — premium in visible areas, durable-standard in private ones.
3. **Finalize the design before execution starts** — mid-work changes are the #1 budget killer.
4. **Get 3D views approved in writing** — "I imagined it differently" after execution is expensive.
5. **Phase it** — do essential rooms now, guest bedroom later. Interiors phase better than structure.

## What a professional interior scope includes

Site measurement, space planning, 3D views, working drawings (electrical, false ceiling, furniture layouts), material/brand selection, and supervised execution with a snag-free handover.
