# Vaastu-Compliant Home Plans in Ajmer: Principles That Actually Work With Modern Design

In Rajasthan, most families want Vaastu compliance in their home — and they're right to ask. The trick is integrating it with genuinely good architecture instead of treating it as a constraint that ruins the plan.

## Core Vaastu principles for home orientation

- **Main entrance:** North, East, or Northeast are considered most auspicious
- **Master bedroom:** Southwest zone — also happens to suit privacy and sun patterns well
- **Kitchen:** Southeast (Agni corner); Northwest is the accepted alternative
- **Pooja room:** Northeast — keep it quiet, clean, and well-lit
- **Toilets/staircase:** best placed in zones that also work practically (South, West, Northwest)

## Common myths, honestly addressed

- **"Vaastu means no modern design"** — false. Orientation and zoning principles coexist perfectly with contemporary architecture.
- **"South-facing plots are bad"** — an oversimplification. What matters is the internal zoning and entrance placement, not a blanket direction ban.
- **"Vaastu correction after construction"** — demolishing and rebuilding is rarely needed; most issues are solved at the planning stage for free.

## Why it must be planned from day one

Vaastu is about **zoning and orientation** — decisions made in the first sketches. Trying to "Vaastu-correct" a finished plan leads to compromised rooms and wasted space. Tell your architect upfront that you want Vaastu compliance; a good one designs it into the logic of the plan.
