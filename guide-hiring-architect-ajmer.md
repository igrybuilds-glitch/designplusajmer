# How to Hire an Architect in Ajmer: The Complete 2026 Guide

## Architect vs contractor vs mistri — what's the difference?

- **Mistri/contractor** builds what you tell them. No design liability.
- **Architect** designs the space, structure coordination, and supervises — legally and professionally accountable for the design.

For anything beyond a single-room addition, an architect pays for itself in avoided mistakes.

## 7 things to check before hiring

1. **Built portfolio, not just renders** — ask for addresses of completed projects you can drive past.
2. **Fee structure in writing** — % of project cost or per-sq-ft; what's included (working drawings? supervision visits? 3D views?).
3. **Structural engineering** — who does it? An architect who "manages" structure without a qualified structural input is a red flag.
4. **Supervision scope** — how many site visits, and what happens when the contractor deviates?
5. **Timeline commitment** — design phase weeks, not "ho jayega".
6. **Client references** — call 2 past clients. Ask: "would you hire them again?"
7. **Vaastu alignment** — in Rajasthan this matters to most families; confirm the architect designs Vaastu-compliant layouts if you want them.

## Fee structures you'll see in Ajmer

- **Percentage of construction cost:** typically 3–8% depending on scope
- **Per sq ft:** roughly ₹25–₹80 per sq ft for design, higher with supervision and 3D
- **Lump sum:** fine for small, well-defined scopes

Get the inclusions list in writing either way.

## Red flags

- No written agreement, only verbal promises
- "Design free with construction" (the cost is hidden in inflated construction rates)
- No site visit before quoting
- Can't show a single completed project in Ajmer
- Asks for 50%+ advance before any drawing

## Questions to ask in the first meeting

1. Show me 3 homes you've completed in Ajmer.
2. What's your exact fee, and what does it include/exclude?
3. Who handles structural design and municipal approvals?
4. How many supervision visits are included?
5. What's your design revision policy?
